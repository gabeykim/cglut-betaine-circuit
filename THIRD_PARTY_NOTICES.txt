THIRD-PARTY SOURCES AND DEPOSIT BOUNDARY

This public repository is separate from the private review supplement.
Do not push the full private review ZIP.

Included source records retain source rights, not the project's blanket license:
- UniProt sequences/annotations: UniProt Consortium, CC BY 4.0 for copyrightable
  database content. https://www.uniprot.org/help/license
- Pfam profiles: InterPro/Pfam, CC0 1.0 for downloadable Pfam data.
  https://interpro-documentation.readthedocs.io/en/latest/license.html
- Genome and RefSeq records: NCBI/INSDC accessions in project/data/manifest.csv
  and code. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/
- PDB entity metadata: entry identifiers and sources preserved in project/data/cache/pdb/.
  https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies
- Henke et al. 2021, doi:10.3390/microorganisms9010204: cached article includes
  its CC BY 4.0 notice and author attribution; source of promoter material.
- Liu et al. 2023, doi:10.1021/acssynbio.3c00246: cached article includes
  CC BY 4.0 notice and author attribution; source of bicistronic design material.
- Pfeifer-Sancar et al. 2013, doi:10.1186/1471-2164-14-888: source supplementary
  tables S1/S3 retained with attribution to the article's Creative Commons terms.
- Other included cached article HTML retains its embedded license/attribution.
  No project license replaces those terms.
- Extracted regulatory-site records retain RegulonDB and PRODORIC provenance;
  only original analysis/design contributions receive the project data license.
- Archived DeepTMHMM inference remains attributed to DeepTMHMM 1.0.57. The
  service/model is not redistributed or rerun by this package.

Excluded from this public repository: cached RegulonDB/PRODORIC API
payloads and REBASE downloads whose redistribution terms were not established
in this review; the Ding et al. noncommercial-license full article; and other
cached abstracts/excerpts without a recorded reusable license. Exact excluded
paths, hashes and source entry points are in excluded_inputs.csv.

These exclusions do not change any candidate sequence or reported result.
The lightweight candidate audit runs without them. The cached-input full rerun
requires restoration of excluded input dependencies. Restore from a lawfully
held review archive with publication_code/restore_review_inputs.py, or obtain
the inputs from their original providers subject to their terms. Current online
responses may differ from historical bytes; do not assume hash equality.

The full 38-file rerun was verified using the private complete cached-input
supplement, not from this filtered public directory alone. Resolve the archive
availability of required excluded inputs before describing the public release
as a self-contained reproduction package.
