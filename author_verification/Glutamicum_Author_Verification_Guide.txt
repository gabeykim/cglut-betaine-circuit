What you are checking
You will start a fresh computation yourself, inspect whether it completed, and
compare five newly generated file hashes with the archived reference hashes.
A SHA-256 hash is a fingerprint of a file's exact bytes. A matching fingerprint
is strong evidence that the files are identical. It does not show that the
biological model, source data or interpretation is correct. The short direct
checks below address selected claims in addition to file repeatability.

This guide was prepared with AI assistance. Its existence is not evidence that
Gabriel Kim has performed the checks. Do not add a completed-verification claim
to the paper until you have run and reviewed the checks and retained the record.
Using the same pipeline is an author-run reproduction, not an independently
implemented analysis. You need not type every command from memory or calculate
SHA-256 by hand. What matters is that you personally run, inspect and understand
the check and accurately describe its scope.

5. Check selected claims directly
A. Open the newly generated project/results/identity_rec.csv without saving
changes. Locate the Cg_MtrA rows for Sal_PhoP and Ec_OmpR. Inspect the identities
and aligned_columns_trimmed fields. Using a calculator, independently evaluate
40/111*100, 54/111*100 and their difference. Rounded to two decimal places these
should be 36.04%, 48.65% and 12.61 percentage points. Check that the manuscript
uses aligned columns, not the shorter-sequence denominator. This checks the
reported arithmetic and labels; it does not validate the alignment biologically.

B. Open p1/changes_from_baseline.csv and compare its added-record identifiers
with p1/proposal_97_NOT_FOR_ORDER.fasta. Check that two added scramble controls
account for 95 becoming 97. Confirm that edits to existing records do not count
as new records. Inspect the two added sequences and their listed parents in the
component and change tables. Record which identifiers you checked.

C. For JXN_ECA_PHOQ_A_m1 in project/designs/junctions_v4.csv, inspect the source
accessions, junction coordinates and protein_seq. The listed protein length is
613 residues and DNA length 1842 bases: 613*3+3 = 1842 including the terminal
stop codon. Locate that identifier in junctions_v4.fasta. Record the length
check; do not treat it as proof that every codon or fusion boundary is correct.

D. Read the genomic-background Results paragraph alongside
reproduction/null_count_editorial_check.json. Check 535264*0.0001 = 53.5264.
Explain in your own words why window-level reference counts cannot establish
significance for the 82 selected, merged sites. If you cannot follow this
