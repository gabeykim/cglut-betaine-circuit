READING THE HISTORICAL REPORTS

The original scientific records are preserved to make the design history
inspectable. Reproducing a report file does not endorse all of its interpretations.
The manuscript and points below supersede conflicting historical narrative.

1. A transcript-start heuristic weight is not a calibrated posterior probability.
   Its benchmark uses sequence contexts anchored to known transcription starts
   and restricts candidate positions to those contexts. It does not establish
   unconstrained genomic prediction accuracy or prove Psyn is leaderless.
   Conditional TC/SD1 changes are hypotheses, not validated fixes.
2. The exact-coordinate operator control uses sites that trained the matrix.
   26/29 coordinate recovery is an implementation check, not held-out sensitivity.
3. The 82 intergenic-background sites are retained members of the original 304.
   They are not a fresh exhaustive genome-wide hit total. Empirical tails are
   not binding probabilities or false-discovery rates. Historical Poisson values
   are not used for biological inference in the manuscript.
4. Sequence similarity is not a cross-phosphorylation probability. Domain
   extraction for comparative triage and Pfam gathering-threshold annotation
   used distinct acceptance logic and must not be conflated.
5. Lack of a strong profile hit does not prove absence of a functional domain.
   A computed heptad register, especially in a low-information transmembrane
   region, does not establish that a chimera will transmit a ligand signal.
6. The lysC motif's downstream edge is 115 bases upstream of the mapped TSS,
   not approximately 30. Binding or regulation at this site is unmeasured.
7. The 97 entries are fragments with shared biological dependencies. They are
   not 97 independent trials or finished strains. Baseline 95 + two added
   matched controls = 97; 41 baseline entries also receive conditional edits.
8. Native PacA and McpX binding evidence exists; it does not validate the proposed
   chimeras. Chemoreceptor/PhoQ coupling has precedent in another ligand/host
   setting. Neither absence of all precedent nor validation of this exact system
   is an appropriate claim.
9. Scanner and test success establishes only the properties actually checked.
   Commercial cloning sites and limited host restriction-related motifs are
   separate screens. Neither establishes transformation success.
10. A historical independent-audit JSON marks scanner/folding reruns false because
    those operations are outside that audit script. Separate logs document the
    subsequent original-scanner and baseline-folding reproduction. Revised P1
    folding remains uncomputed.
11. Three phasing controls change background or spacer sequence while keeping
    operator identity and spacing fixed. The earlier table label "operator swaps"
    was incorrect. The 14-position substitution series preserves total length and
    GC content; local sequence and individual nucleotide counts can vary. Read
    historical composition-invariance language with this qualification.
